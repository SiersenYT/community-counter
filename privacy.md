# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 26, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AmethystCPVP Community Counter handles information when you use the application to display streaming community statistics.

## 2. Information accessed

When you connect supported accounts, the application may access information permitted by your authorization, such as:

- YouTube subscriber counts.
- Twitch follower counts.
- TikTok follower counts.

The application requests access only to the information needed for its supported functionality.

## 3. Authorization and storage

The application uses authorization mechanisms provided by the relevant platforms.

Authorization tokens may be stored locally on your Windows device using Windows Credential Manager to maintain account connections. These tokens are sensitive and should not be shared.

The application uses the relevant platform APIs to retrieve authorized account information. This policy does not imply that no data is transmitted: API requests are sent to the services you connect.

## 4. Sharing and disclosure

The application is designed to display community statistics in a streaming overlay. If you broadcast that overlay, the displayed information may be visible to your audience.

We do not claim that third-party platforms do not collect information when you use their services. Their own privacy policies apply to their handling of your data.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

Authorization credentials may remain stored on your device until you disconnect an account, remove the stored credentials, or uninstall or reset the relevant application components.

You can revoke the application's access through the connected platform's account settings.

## 6. Security

Reasonable precautions should be taken to protect stored authorization credentials and your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Third-party services

YouTube, Twitch, and TikTok operate independently and have their own privacy policies and data-handling practices.

## 8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

## 9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: **amethystcpvp@gmail.com**.
